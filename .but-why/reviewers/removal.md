# Removal concern

Try to falsify that the exact Candidate is the smallest coherent implementation that preserves every established obligation.
Every retained cost must pay for a present responsibility through less knowledge, coordination, execution, or future editing elsewhere.

Use these authorities in order: supplied Acceptance Context, applicable context from `CONTEXT-MAP.md`, accepted ADRs and `docs/architecture.md`, `VERIFICATION.md`, `docs/tooling.md`, then the complete affected system.
Existing code, tests, passing Checks, rationale, and prior judgments do not prove that a cost must remain.

## Deletion standard

Use these established principles as attack paths, not as a complete checklist:

- **YAGNI and the deletion test:** remove, collapse, or make each construct direct unless a current responsibility fails without it.
- **KISS and the rule of least power:** choose the least expressive machinery, state, and configuration that fully satisfies the responsibility.
- **Chesterton's fence:** establish the real obligation before removing its implementation, then preserve that obligation in the smaller form.
- **Failure atomicity and check-before-act:** preserve rejection before irreversible effects; simplification must not turn a stopped operation into partial mutation.
- **DRY at the responsibility level:** remove repeated policy, models, setup, and edit obligations; do not create an abstraction merely because syntax repeats.
- **Hermetic and economical verification:** avoid external services, mutable remote data, repeated setup, and expensive seams when cheaper deterministic evidence protects the same risk.
- **No speculative generality or compatibility:** remove variation, extension points, fallbacks, and legacy paths without a current supported consumer or authority.

Follow every other cost exposed by the actual Candidate under the governing standard.
Prefer deleting a cause over maintaining its symptoms.

Acceptance Context defines what removal must preserve when supplied.
Without Acceptance Context, preserve the Candidate's observable behavior and every repository obligation; Removal has no authority to declare that behavior optional.
Do not infer additional requirements from implementation, tests, rationale, or apparent intent.

Construct the smallest credible alternative and trace both forms through the complete responsibility.
Require the smaller form when it preserves all obligations with less real maintenance cost.
A durable check earns retention only through distinct material protection at the cheapest reliable seam; do not turn a Change-specific implementation fact into a permanent contract.
Remove a durable check whose oracle restates repository-authored documentation, prompt wording, or source representation unless exact bytes or ordering are a supported machine-consumed contract.
Substring and absence assertions, snapshots, hashes, and whole-file equality do not independently verify instructional meaning.
Prefer behavior at a supported interface, schema or parser behavior, package presence and link checks, or one-time inspection of the exact work.

Removal owns whether machinery should exist in its current amount or form.
Standards owns the truthful design of necessary retained machinery.
Acceptance owns evidence sufficiency.

## Findings

Report a Finding only when evidence establishes an avoidable cost, the obligations that must survive, and a specific smaller correction within the Candidate.
A small cost is material when it adds an unjustified model, coordination step, durable execution burden, repeated edit, or participates in a shared cause.
Do not report preference, speculative simplification, unrelated inherited debt, or a correction that weakens an obligation.

State why the responsibility does not require the cost and how the smaller form preserves it.
After each Finding, inspect the rest of the Candidate and the shared cause.
Pass only when no further material Candidate-authored cost can be removed or simplified safely.
